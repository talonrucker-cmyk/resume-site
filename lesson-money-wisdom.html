01 — SPEND UNDER YOUR MEANS
Principle 2 · Put money to work

Trade hours for dollars once — then make dollars work.

Active income tops out

A paycheck stops the moment you do. There are only so many hours to sell, so income from work alone always hits a ceiling.

Assets pay while you sleep

Money invested in the market becomes an asset that earns on its own — growth and dividends that keep coming whether you’re working, sleeping, or on a beach.

The gap is the seed

The difference between what you earn and what you spend is what you invest. Live under your means, and that gap quietly buys you freedom.

“Rich isn’t a big paycheck. It’s owning enough assets that they out-earn your job.”

The market averages ~10% over decades — but almost no single year is 10%. It’s +25% and −15%, not a smooth line.

Why plan low

Build your plan on ~6–8%, not 10%, and definitely not last year’s hot number. If reality does better, you’re early. If you assume too much, you fall short right when it matters.

06 — SEQUENCE & DOLLAR-COST AVERAGING
Principle 4 · The discipline

Buy on a schedule, not on a feeling.

Same dollars, every time

Dollar-cost averaging means investing a fixed amount on a set schedule — every paycheck, every month — no matter what the market is doing.

You buy more when it’s cheap

The fixed dollars buy more shares when prices are low and fewer when high. A crash becomes a sale, not a disaster.

It beats your emotions

The real enemy is panic-selling low and piling in high. Automating your buys takes the decision — and the fear — out of your hands.

“Time in the market beats timing the market — and dollar-cost averaging is how normal people stay in.”
07 — DOLLAR-COST AVERAGING
The throughline

Wise with money, in four moves.

Spend

Under your means

The car, the house, the lifestyle — keep them below what you earn. The gap you protect is the money that builds everything else.

Invest

Into assets that pay

Turn that gap into market investments that grow and pay you daily — on a realistic ~6–8%, not a fantasy number.

Stay

Steady and unfooled

Don’t trust a flashy average, don’t time the market, don’t panic in a crash. Buy on a schedule and let time do the heavy lifting.

Nobody gets wealthy from one great year. They get wealthy by spending less than they make and staying invested through every kind of year.
